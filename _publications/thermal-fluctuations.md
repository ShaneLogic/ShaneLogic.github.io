summary: >-
  Finite-temperature first-principles calculations examine the contribution of thermal
  fluctuations to the hole effective mass of CsPbBr3. The results show why finite-temperature
  lattice motion must be considered when interpreting effective-mass measurements
  and separating thermal effects from evidence for polaron formation.
